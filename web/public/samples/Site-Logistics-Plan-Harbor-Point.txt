BM-102 HARBOR POINT MIXED-USE TOWER - SITE LOGISTICS PLAN

1. DELIVERIES
All deliveries must be scheduled through the logistics coordinator at least 48 hours in advance. Delivery hours are 7:00 AM to 3:30 PM on weekdays. No deliveries are permitted on Alaskan Way during the Friday ferry peak between 3:30 PM and 6:30 PM.

2. TOWER CRANE
The tower crane is shared between the concrete, curtain wall and elevator trades. Crane time is allocated at the Thursday logistics meeting. Picks over the public sidewalk require a city street-use permit and a flagger at each end of the closure.

3. HOIST
The personnel and material hoist runs from 6:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Material loads above 4,000 lb must use the crane. The hoist will be removed when the permanent elevators are accepted, currently forecast for December 2026.

4. NOISE AND NEIGHBORS
Work generating noise above 80 dBA at the property line is limited to 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Concrete pours that must start earlier require a noise variance from the city at least 10 days in advance.

5. WASTE
Waste is separated into wood, metal, drywall, cardboard and general debris to meet the 75% diversion target for LEED. Dumpster swaps occur before 7:00 AM.
